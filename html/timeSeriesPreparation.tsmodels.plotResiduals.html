timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.plotResiduals

Draw the four-panel residual check from tsCheckResiduals.

Syntax

h = timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.plotResiduals(out)

Description

The drawing half of sigtools' tsCheckResiduals, which always drew in v1.3.1, split at X-02 (owner decision 2026-09-23). It draws the figure v1.3.1 drew, in four panels: - the standardised residuals over time; - the residual ACF with the +/- white-noise band; - the normal Q-Q plot, titled with the Jarque-Bera p-value; - the Ljung-Box p-value ladder with the 5 % line.

Inputs

Outputs

namesizetypedefaultdescription

Accuracy

Not applicable, because the function draws and computes nothing.

Errors

identifiercausecondition
timeSeriesPreparation:plotResiduals:badInputInputout lacks a field of tsCheckResiduals' output.

Example

An AR(1) fit, checked and drawn.

x = filter(1, [1 -0.6], randn(RandStream("twister", "Seed", 2), 500, 1));
out = timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.tsCheckResiduals(timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.tsFitAR(x, 1));
h = timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.plotResiduals(out);
close(h.figure);

Limitations

Always a new figure.
