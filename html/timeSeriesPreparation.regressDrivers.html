timeSeriesPreparation.regressDrivers

Regression of per-revolution parameters on drivers, with a VIF gate and block-bootstrap sigmas.

Syntax

res = timeSeriesPreparation.regressDrivers(P, Z)
res = timeSeriesPreparation.regressDrivers(P, Z, Names = [], DriverNames = [], MaxVif = 10, NBoot = 1000, BlockLength = [], Seed = 0, Stage = "regressDrivers", Level = 0.6827)

Description

The regression of CONCEPT 7: gated per-revolution parameters (a bias or scale per revolution, from the application's fit) on the drivers averaged per revolution by the caller. Rows are revolutions in time order, so that blocks of rows are blocks of time. - VIF gate: vif = diag(inv(corrcoef(Z))) on the rows complete in Z. A driver above MaxVif refuses (default 10, the common rule of thumb, Kutner et al. 2005; owner decision 2026-09-24, provisional); a caller that accepts collinear drivers raises MaxVif. - For each parameter j, ordinary least squares on the rows complete in P(:,j) and Z, with the drivers demeaned over those rows (CONCEPT 8: demeaned regressors decorrelate the intercept): P(:,j) = c0 + (Z - mean(Z)) c + e. coef(1,j) = c0, the mean of P(:,j); coef(2:end,j) = c. - sigma and ci: moving-block bootstrap of the rows [P(:,j) Z] (bootstrap; automatic Politis-White block length unless BlockLength; seed from Stage and Seed), each replicate refitted with its own demeaning (CONCEPT 8: naive sigmas were 5-14 times optimistic on GRACE residuals). - r2: 1 - SSE/SST of the fit.

Accuracy

coef is the least-squares solution by QR (backslash); with exact data it recovers the coefficients to rounding. The bootstrap slope sigma lay within 0.86-1.21 of the OLS sigma for white errors (NumPy, 100 realisations of 600 rows, 2026-09-24); for serially correlated errors it is the one that stays honest. The intercept's bootstrap sigma also carries the scatter of the replicate means of Z.

Example

A bias per revolution driven by beta' and the eclipse fraction.

rs = RandStream("twister", "Seed", 21);
Z = [randn(rs, 300, 1), rand(rs, 300, 1)];
P = 2 + Z * [0.3; -0.5] + 0.1 * randn(rs, 300, 1);
res = timeSeriesPreparation.regressDrivers(P, Z, DriverNames = ["betaPrime" "eclipse"], NBoot = 200);

Limitations

Linear in the drivers, one parameter at a time. The bootstrap percentile interval is first-order accurate. Per-revolution averaging and gating are the caller's.
