timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.plotPsd2armaFit

Draw a psd2arma fit: target and fitted PSD, and the fit error in dB.

Syntax

h = timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.plotPsd2armaFit(results)

Description

The drawing half of sigtools' psd2arma (its former Display option), split at X-02 (owner decision 2026-09-23). It draws two panels in one figure: - top: the target PSD and the fitted PSD on log-log axes, titled with the orders and the RMS and maximum errors; - bottom: the error 10 log10(PSDfit/PSDtarget) in dB over frequency. The zero-frequency bin is left out of the log-log panel.

Inputs

namesizetypedefaultdescription
results1,1struct—- Third output of psd2arma.

Outputs

namesizetypedefaultdescription
h1,1struct—- Graphics handles: figure, psdAxes, errorAxes.

Accuracy

Not applicable, because the function draws. The error panel divides the stored PSDfit by PSDtarget; psd2arma floors both at 1e-12 of the target's peak before its own RMSErrorDb, which the title quotes, so the panel can differ from it only where the fit falls below that floor.

Errors

identifiercausecondition
timeSeriesPreparation:plotPsd2armaFit:badResultsInputresults lacks a field of psd2arma's output.

Example

The psd2arma example, drawn.

f = logspace(-2, 2, 500)';
[~, ~, res] = timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.psd2arma(f, 1 ./ (1 + (f/5).^2).^2, 500, Order = [8 8]);
h = timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.plotPsd2armaFit(res);
close(h.figure);

Limitations

Always a new figure.
