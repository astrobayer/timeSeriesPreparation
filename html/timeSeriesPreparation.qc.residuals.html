timeSeriesPreparation.qc.residuals

Residuals of measured minus modelled observations under a preliminary calibration, optionally whitened.

Syntax

r = timeSeriesPreparation.qc.residuals(y_meas, y_model)
[r, info] = timeSeriesPreparation.qc.residuals(y_meas, y_model, Bias = b, Scale = S, Whitening = "highpass", Window = 601)

Description

r_raw = y_meas - (b + y_model * S'), per channel (columns), with the caller's preliminary calibration (defaults b = 0, S = I: the plain difference). Whitening prepares the residual for the detectors, which assume white noise: "none" r = r_raw; "difference" r = diff(r_raw), NaN in the first row: flattens the 1/f accelerometer noise, doubles the white-noise variance, turns a level jump into a one-sample impulse; "highpass" r = r_raw - movmean(r_raw, Window, "omitnan"): removes bias drift slower than Window samples and keeps jumps and pulses shorter than Window/2 (the choice for detectJumps). NaN samples propagate (they never enter the moving mean) and are never invented; the detectors handle them. Ported from NonGravForces.qc 0.12.0 (X-05); numbers unchanged (X-05b).

Accuracy

Arithmetic only. "highpass" keeps h (1 - 1/2) of a step of height h at its centre over Window/2 samples on either side; "difference" is exact for a step (one sample of height h). The robust sigma is consistent with the standard deviation for Gaussian noise; for 1/f noise it overestimates by up to 10 % inside short windows.

Example

Residuals of three channels under a preliminary scale, high-passed.

rs = RandStream("twister", "Seed", 1);
y = randn(rs, 1000, 3); m = 0.9 * y + 0.1 * randn(rs, 1000, 3);
[r, info] = timeSeriesPreparation.qc.residuals(y, m, Scale = [1.1 1.1 1.1], Whitening = "highpass", Window = 61);

Limitations

The preliminary calibration is the caller's; nothing is fitted here.
