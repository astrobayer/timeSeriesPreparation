timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.tsAcf

Sample autocorrelation function (gap-aware).

Syntax

[rho, lags, conf, nPairs] = timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.tsAcf(x)
[rho, lags, conf, nPairs] = timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.tsAcf(x, maxLag)

Description

The sample ACF is the first look at a series before an ARMA model is identified: rho(k) = c(k)/c(0) at lags 0..maxLag, rho(1) = 1, with the observed-sample mean removed.

A complete series gets the textbook biased estimator, c(k) = sum x(t) x(t+k) / N. A series with NaN at missing epochs of an otherwise regular grid is handled gap-aware: each lag k uses only the pairs (t, t+k) in which both samples are observed, divided by their count (a consistent moment estimator). A lag without any such pair is NaN. conf is the approximate 95 % white-noise band 1.96/sqrt(n), with n the lag-1 pair count (N for a complete series). Base MATLAB only.

Accuracy

Exact arithmetic of the estimator, no approximation beyond rounding. The band is the large-sample normal approximation (Box, Jenkins, Reinsel). Unchanged from sigtools v1.3.1; TestSigtoolsEquivalence holds it bit for bit.

Example

The ACF of an AR(1) series decays like 0.7^k.

x = filter(1, [1 -0.7], randn(RandStream("twister", "Seed", 1), 1000, 1));
[rho, lags, conf] = timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.tsAcf(x, 10);

Limitations

The gap-aware ACF is not guaranteed positive-definite; under heavy gapping tsPacf can then exceed 1 in magnitude. A series whose observed samples are all equal returns rho = ones and conf from the number observed. Irregular sampling is not supported: NaN must mark missing epochs of a regular grid.
