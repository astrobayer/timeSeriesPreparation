timeSeriesPreparation.binnedMap

Mean, standard deviation and count of a value in bins of two coordinates.

Syntax

map = timeSeriesPreparation.binnedMap(value, a, b, edgesA, edgesB)

Description

The binned map of CONCEPT 7, e.g. a residual over (beta', u). Each sample falls in the bin of its a and b by discretize's rule: bins are [e_i, e_(i+1)), the last one closed [e_(n-1), e_n]. Samples with NaN in value, a or b, and samples outside the edges, are ignored. Per bin: the mean, the standard deviation (normalised by n - 1; NaN below two samples) and the count. Empty bins have mean NaN (owner decision 2026-09-24). The edges are the caller's: a bin width is physics, not a default (R5).

Inputs

bN,1double—- Second coordinate, e.g. u in deg.

edgesB1,:double—- Bin edges of b, likewise.

Outputs

namesizetypedefaultdescription

Accuracy

Exact accumulation per bin (accumarray); TestBinnedMap holds it against an explicit loop over the bins to 1e-12.

Errors

identifiercausecondition
timeSeriesPreparation:binnedMap:sizeMismatchInputvalue, a and b are not vectors of one length.
timeSeriesPreparation:binnedMap:badEdgesInputan edge vector has fewer than two entries, is not strictly increasing, or is not finite.

Example

A residual over beta' and u in 10 x 30 degree bins.

rs = RandStream("twister", "Seed", 23);
beta = 180 * rand(rs, 5000, 1) - 90;
u = 360 * rand(rs, 5000, 1);
r = 1e-9 * sind(u) .* cosd(beta) + 1e-10 * randn(rs, 5000, 1);
map = timeSeriesPreparation.binnedMap(r, beta, u, -90:10:90, 0:30:360);

Limitations

No wrap-around: an angle such as u must be mapped into the edges' range by the caller (u = 360 falls in the last bin, 0 in the first). No weighting and no robust statistics.
