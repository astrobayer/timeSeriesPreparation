timeSeriesPreparation.qc.detectImpulses

Matched-filter detection of pulses of a known shape in whitened residuals; thruster priors are assigned first.

Syntax

[events, info] = timeSeriesPreparation.qc.detectImpulses(r, Epoch = t, Response = h)
[events, info] = timeSeriesPreparation.qc.detectImpulses(r, Epoch = t, Response = h, Priors = thr, Threshold = 5, PriorWindow_s = 5)

Description

Each channel is correlated with the zero-mean, unit-norm template hz = (h - mean h)/|h - mean h| (L samples, the pulse starting at its first sample): c(t) = sum_i r(t+i-1) hz(i), score = c/sigma_c, with sigma_c the robust (1.4826 MAD) scale of c. The zero-mean template is blind to an offset or a level jump inside the window; only a step's edge can register, once at most. The last L-1 samples are not evaluable. Local maxima of |score| above Threshold, at least L samples apart (the strongest wins), are pulses with amplitude a = c/(hz.h), sigma_a = sigma_c/(hz.h), tStart at the peak and tEnd L-1 samples later. A pulse within PriorWindow_s of a prior row (e.g. THR1B firings) takes the prior's source and confidence 1; the others are "unassigned" with confidence erf(|score|/sqrt(2)). NaN samples count as zero in the correlation and are reported. Ported from NonGravForces.qc 0.12.0 (X-05); numbers unchanged (X-05b, X-16).

Accuracy

Planted pulses are recovered to +-1 sample and their amplitude to 3 sigma_a above Threshold (measured in the ported tests). Two pulses closer than L samples merge into the stronger one. A template that does not match the true shape lowers the score by the cosine between the shapes, never raises it.

Example

One raised-cosine pulse of 8 sigma in white noise.

x = 1e-9 * randn(RandStream("twister", "Seed", 3), 3000, 1);
h = sin(pi * (0:29)' / 30).^2; x(1000:1029) = x(1000:1029) + 8e-9 * h;
t = datetime(2020, 1, 1, "TimeZone", "UTC") + seconds((0:2999)');
events = timeSeriesPreparation.qc.detectImpulses(x, Epoch = t, Response = h);

Limitations

Pulses shorter than the template are unresolvable in L1B. The template is the caller's; the CRN impulse response is crnKernel's.
