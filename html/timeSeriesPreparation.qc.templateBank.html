timeSeriesPreparation.qc.templateBank

Empirical spike templates of the ACT1B residual, per class and satellite.

Syntax

bank = timeSeriesPreparation.qc.templateBank()

Description

The labelled template bank of QC (CONCEPT 6.1 row 2, X-17; owner decisions 2026-09-26). Each template is the median stack of the GRACE-FO ACT1B residual (lin_accl minus its 301-s moving median) around real events, measured by the throwaway scan PR #62 on the DATAPOOL months 2020-01 and 2020-07, the event-weighted mean of the two months. One class so far: thruster around every THR1B record with an on-time: C 27 117 events, D 24 551. y dips by 1.2e-8 (C) / 1.5e-8 (D) m/s^2 at the record, z rises by 2.1e-9 / 2.2e-9 m/s^2; main lobe about +-15 s with CRN sidelobes. The two months agree to 6 % of the peak. Not in the bank (HANDOVER X-17): magnetic torquer (a coupling to continuous currents, N-11), heater (no key series in L1B RL04), broad z-spikes (ignored, owner decision).

Inputs

(none)

Outputs

namesizetypedefaultdescription
bank1,1struct—- Field per class ("thruster"), each a struct with fields lags (121,1) s, -60..60; C and D, the (121,3) template in m/s^2 on x, y, z; nEvents (1,1) struct with C and D; source (1,1) string.

Accuracy

Stack medians of 24 551 to 27 117 events; the month-to-month difference is below 9e-10 m/s^2 (6 % of the peak). The x column is 0 on C: more than a third of the C x residual is exactly 0 (HANDOVER X-17).

Errors

(none) - the bank is data; it does not refuse.

Example

The y peak of the thruster template of GRACE-FO D.

b = timeSeriesPreparation.qc.templateBank();
peak = min(b.thruster.D(:, 2));

Limitations

ACT1B at 1 Hz only; ACC1A templates follow with X-12. Two months of GRACE-FO C and D; classic GRACE after G-02.
