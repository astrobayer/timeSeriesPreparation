timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.plotAllanVariance

Draw Allan deviation on log-log axes with its shaded confidence band.

Syntax

h = timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.plotAllanVariance(tau, adev, info)
h = timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.plotAllanVariance(tau, adev, info, CiLevel = 0.95)

Description

The drawing half of sigtools' allanVariance (its former doPlot), split at X-02 (owner decision 2026-09-23). Each column of adev is one line over tau, in log-log axes, with the band between info.adevLow and info.adevHigh shaded. The figure is the one v1.3.1 drew. CiLevel only labels the title and must be the level the bounds were computed with.

Accuracy

Errors

Example

White noise, the default 1-sigma band.

x = randn(RandStream("twister", "Seed", 1), 10000, 1);
[tau, ~, adev, info] = timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.allanVariance(x, 10);
h = timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.plotAllanVariance(tau, adev, info);
close(h.figure);

Limitations

Draws what it is given; it cannot check that CiLevel matches the bounds.
