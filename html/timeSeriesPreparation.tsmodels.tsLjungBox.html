timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.tsLjungBox

Ljung-Box portmanteau test for residual autocorrelation.

Syntax

[Q, pValue, df] = timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.tsLjungBox(resid)
[Q, pValue, df] = timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.tsLjungBox(resid, lags)
[Q, pValue, df] = timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.tsLjungBox(resid, lags, nParam)

Description

Tests whether the residuals of a fitted model are white, jointly over the first L lags: H0 says the innovations are uncorrelated up to lag L. NaN residuals (missing epochs) and leading exact zeros (the conditioning samples of a CSS fit) are dropped, the mean is removed, and with r_k the sample ACF of tsAcf, Q = N (N + 2) sum_{k=1..L} r_k^2 / (N - k), which under H0 is chi-square with df = max(L - nParam, 1) degrees of freedom, nParam = p + q the fitted ARMA parameters. pValue is the upper tail 1 - chi2cdf(Q, df); a small value rejects whiteness. Base MATLAB only: the chi-square CDF is the regularised incomplete gamma function, by series (x < a + 1) or Lentz continued fraction.

Inputs

resid:,1double—- Residual series; NaN marks a missing epoch.
lags1,1double—- Number of lags L tested; [] or omitted: 20. Rounded and capped at N - 1.
nParam1,1double—- Fitted parameter count p + q, subtracted from the degrees of freedom; [] or omitted: 0.

Outputs

Accuracy

The incomplete gamma series and continued fraction stop at a relative change below 1e-14 (at most 1000 terms), as in tsCheckResiduals. The chi-square law of Q is asymptotic (Ljung and Box 1978). Unchanged from sigtools v1.3.1; TestSigtoolsEquivalence holds it bit for bit.

Errors

(none) A non-numeric or complex resid, or a lags or nParam that is not a numeric scalar or [], is refused by the arguments block with a MATLAB: identifier.

Example

White noise: the p-value is typically large.

e = randn(RandStream("twister", "Seed", 3), 500, 1);
[Q, pValue, df] = timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.tsLjungBox(e, 20);

Limitations

Every exact zero before the first nonzero residual is dropped, a genuine zero included. With gaps the ACF is pairwise (tsAcf) and N counts the observed residuals only.

References

G. M. Ljung, G. E. P. Box (1978), Biometrika 65(2), 297-303. G. E. P. Box, G. M. Jenkins, G. C. Reinsel, Time Series Analysis: Forecasting and Control, Wiley.
