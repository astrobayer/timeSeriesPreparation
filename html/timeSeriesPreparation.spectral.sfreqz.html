timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.sfreqz

Digital frequency response of an IIR/FIR filter (base MATLAB).

Syntax

H = timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.sfreqz(b, a, w)
[H, w] = timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.sfreqz(b, a, f, Fs)

Description

Evaluates the complex response H = B(z)/A(z) of a rational filter on a supplied grid, as the Signal Processing Toolbox freqz does, but in base MATLAB. The polynomials are in ascending powers of z^-1, B(z) = b(1) + b(2) z^-1 + ..., A(z) = a(1) + a(2) z^-1 + ..., and are evaluated at z^-1 = exp(-1i w) by accumulating powers. With Fs, the third argument is read as frequencies in Hz and converted by w = 2 pi f/Fs. |A| below 1e-300 is clamped to 1e-300, so a pole on the grid gives a huge but finite value. Real or complex coefficients work, and the class of w (single, gpuArray) is kept. No grid is generated and nothing is drawn. psd2arma and arma2psd evaluate their models with it.

Inputs

namesizetypedefaultdescription
b1,nb+1double—- Numerator coefficients [b0 ... b_nb].
a1,na+1double—- Denominator coefficients [a0 ... a_na]; a0 is usually 1.
wM,1double—rad/sa Angular frequencies, typically in [0, pi]; in Hz when Fs is given.
Fs1,1double—Hz Optional sampling rate, > 0. If given (and not []), w is converted from Hz.

Outputs

namesizetypedefaultdescription
HM,1double—- Complex frequency response at each w.
wM,1double—rad/sa Angular frequencies actually used.

Accuracy

Direct evaluation of the two polynomials; the error is that of the power accumulation, which grows with the order. sigtools' HANDOFF states no separate number for it; its fits (sinvfreqz, sstmcb) are checked through it. Unchanged from sigtools v1.3.1; TestSigtoolsEquivalence holds it bit for bit.

Errors

(none) A non-numeric input, or an Fs that is not a positive finite scalar, is refused by the arguments block with a MATLAB: identifier.

Example

Response of an ARMA(2,2) filter on 512 points from 0 to pi.

w = linspace(0, pi, 512)';
H = timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.sfreqz([1 -0.5 0.2], [1 -1.2 0.6], w);

Limitations

Evaluates on a given grid only (no automatic grid, no plot). The power accumulation loses accuracy for very high orders; factor such filters into sections.

References
