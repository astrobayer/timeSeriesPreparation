timeSeriesPreparation.qc.screen

Quality-control orchestrator: residuals, outliers, impulses, jumps, then the mask and the event table.

Syntax

[mask, events, info] = timeSeriesPreparation.qc.screen(y_meas, y_model, epoch)
[mask, events, info] = timeSeriesPreparation.qc.screen(y_meas, y_model, epoch, Response = crn, Thrusters = thr, Priors = ecl)

Description

1. r = qc.residuals(y_meas, y_model, Bias, Scale, Whitening, Window). 2. Outliers: qc.hampel(r, HampelWindow, HampelThreshold). 3. Impulses, skipped (and said so in info) without a Response, since no pulse shape is assumed: qc.detectImpulses on the residual with only the isolated outliers (runs of at most 2 samples) replaced by the local median. Hampel also flags the peaks of a strong pulse, in runs of several samples; replacing those would destroy the pulse, while a one- or two-sample spike must not reach the matched filter. Detected pulses claim their samples (their outlier flags are withdrawn) and are blanked before the jump search. 4. Jumps: qc.detectJumps with JumpModel, JumpPenalty, JumpMinSegment and Priors, on the residual with outliers replaced and pulses blanked. mask marks outliers and the samples spanned by impulses; jumps are reported, never masked. events is the three detectors' event table, sorted by tStart. Ported from NonGravForces.qc 0.12.0 (X-05); numbers unchanged (X-05b, X-16).

Accuracy

The composition of the four units; see theirs. A level step may register once as an impulse candidate at its edge; the jump is then located at the end of that window, up to L samples late. On the ported tests' synthetic day (2 pulses, 1 step, 1 spike in 3000 white samples) the spike and both pulses are masked and the step is reported once.

Example

A spike and a step in white noise, no pulse template.

x = 1e-9 * randn(RandStream("twister", "Seed", 4), 3000, 1);
x(700) = 3e-8; x(2500:end) = x(2500:end) + 5e-9;
t = datetime(2020, 1, 1, "TimeZone", "UTC") + seconds((0:2999)');
[mask, events] = timeSeriesPreparation.qc.screen(x, zeros(3000, 1), t);

Limitations

One arc at a time; the order of the steps is fixed.
