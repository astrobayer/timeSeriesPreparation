timeSeriesPreparation.qc.hampel

Robust point-outlier detector: moving median and scaled MAD, per column.

Syntax

mask = timeSeriesPreparation.qc.hampel(x)
[mask, info] = timeSeriesPreparation.qc.hampel(x, Window = 61, Threshold = 4, MinSigma = 0)

Description

Flags sample i of column j when |x(i,j) - med(i,j)| > t * 1.4826 * MAD(i,j), with med and MAD over a centred window of W samples, truncated at the edges. Median and MAD tolerate up to about half the window contaminated, which is why this, not a moving mean and std, is the first pass on residuals with thruster spikes and twangs. NaN samples are never flagged and never enter the window statistics; a window with fewer than three finite samples flags nothing. A row vector is one channel. Ported from NonGravForces.qc 0.12.0 (X-05); numbers unchanged (X-05b, X-16).

Accuracy

1.4826 makes the MAD consistent with sigma for Gaussian noise; for 1/f accelerometer noise it overestimates sigma by up to about 10 % inside a 61-sample window, which is conservative. Impulses wider than Window/2 are not outliers here by design; they go to detectImpulses.

Example

One planted outlier in white noise.

x = randn(RandStream("twister", "Seed", 2), 1000, 1); x(500) = 12;
mask = timeSeriesPreparation.qc.hampel(x);

Limitations

O(K W) per channel, vectorised (X-16). A full, finite window has an odd count, so its median is its middle element: movmedian gives it without arithmetic. The MAD is one median over the windows, gathered in cache-sized blocks of at most 2^16 elements. Edge windows and windows with NaN take median with "omitnan". The outputs equal the X-05 port's per-sample loop bit for bit (TestQcEquivalence).
