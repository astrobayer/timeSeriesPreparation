timeSeriesPreparation.noise

Residual noise model per axis: ARMA by exact ML with gaps, or PSD -> ARMA.

Syntax

nm = timeSeriesPreparation.noise(arc, order)
nm = timeSeriesPreparation.noise(arc, order, Method = "exact", MaxLag = 100, Lags = 20, WelchAverages = 32, Seed = 0, Stage = "noise")

Description

The noise stage (CONCEPT 6.1, row 5): an ARMA(p,q) model of each column of arc, for the gap stage and for the covariance of the applications' lsqvce fit. The numerics are the sigtools functions, unchanged; this function applies them to an Arc, one axis at a time: - rows with weight 0 or filled are set to NaN (CONCEPT 5); - Method "exact": tsmodels.tsFitArima(x, p, 0, q), exact Gaussian ML by a Kalman filter that skips the NaN rows; - Method "psd": a Welch spectrum of WelchAverages Hann segments (spectral.powerSpectrum, 50 % overlap) and spectral.psd2arma with Order [p q]. psd2arma sets the gain by a log least-squares fit, which on a raw periodogram (chi-square, 2 degrees of freedom) biases sigma2 low by exp(-0.577) = 0.56; averaging makes that bias about -1/dof (dof about 2 WelchAverages). An axis with missing rows would need an unaveraged Lomb-Scargle periodogram, so "psd" refuses it; use "exact" there; - the autocovariance of the model, spectral.arma2acv (exact, Lyapunov), at lags 0..MaxLag samples; - the Ljung-Box test of the model's residuals over Lags lags, tsmodels.tsLjungBox with p + q fitted parameters. The model is x_t - mu = filter(b, a, e), e white of variance sigma2, b(1) = a(1) = 1: a = [1, -phi], b = [1, theta] in tsFitArima's terms.

The order is the caller's choice; no default is assumed (R5). tsFitArima perturbs its starting points with randn. Every axis therefore starts from the same state of an mrg32k3a stream seeded by internal.stageSeed(Stage, Seed) (CONCEPT 8): an axis is fitted identically alone and in a batched call, and reruns are identical. The caller's global stream is restored afterwards.

Accuracy

That of the functions applied (see their help). "exact" is maximum-likelihood efficient; for an AR(1) the standard error of phi is sqrt((1 - phi^2)/n). "psd" inherits the scatter of the spectrum estimate and psd2arma's fit error; it is the fast route for long arcs. acv is exact for the fitted model.

Example

An AR(1) at 1 Hz with a gap: phi near 0.7, residuals white.

aux = struct("obsNames", {{'lin_accl_x'}}, "variableNames", {{'lin_accl_x'}}, "variableFieldNames", {{'lin_accl_x'}}, "variableUnits", struct("lin_accl_x", "m/s2"), "fileName", "ACT1B_2020-01-01_C_04.txt", "product", "ACT1B");
x = filter(1, [1 -0.7], randn(RandStream("twister", "Seed", 5), 1500, 1));
x(400:450) = NaN;
nm = timeSeriesPreparation.noise(timeSeriesPreparation.Arc(int64(0:1499)' * int64(1e9), x, aux), [1 0], MaxLag = 10);

Limitations

Stationary ARMA only; differencing and seasonal terms are the caller's (tsmodels.tsFitSarima). "psd" needs gap-free axes; its residuals are formed by inverse filtering. Allan deviation (spectral.allanVariance) needs a gap-free stretch and is not computed here.
