timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.spec2acv

One- or two-sided PSD/ASD to auto-covariance / -correlation.

Syntax

[R, lags] = timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.spec2acv(f, S)
[R, lags, info] = timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.spec2acv(f, S, Method = "direct", Lags = (0:10)'/100, Window = "tukey")

Description

Transforms a spectral density S on the frequency grid f into the auto-covariance R(tau) through the Wiener-Khinchin theorem, R(tau) = integral_0^fNyq S(f) cos(2 pi f tau) df for a one-sided PSD. Measured spectra rarely sit on a uniform grid: non-uniform, offset, unsorted or log-spaced grids are sorted, duplicates averaged, and the spectrum resampled (pchip, in linear or log-log space) onto a uniform DC-aligned grid; below the lowest frequency S is zero-padded and above the highest it is taken as zero. Both methods use the trapezoidal rule with half weight at DC and Nyquist, so with Window "none" and Smooth "none" R(0) equals trapz(f,S) exactly, and "ifft" and "direct" agree to rounding. "ifft" is fast with the fixed lag step dt = 1/(2 fNyq); "direct" evaluates arbitrary Lags with NUFFT when available (R2020a+), otherwise with an O(N L) cosine matrix. Optional spectral smoothing (fixed-width kernels, or constant-relative-bandwidth "octave" smoothing for wideband ASDs) and a spectral taper w(f), w(0) = 1, limit ringing. With DOF (the chi-square degrees of freedom of the PSD estimate, e.g. about 2 nAvg for Welch) the 1-sigma uncertainty of R is propagated to info.Rstd, including the variance reduction of the smoothing. Single and gpuArray inputs are preserved.

Accuracy

With Window "none" and Smooth "none", R(0) equals trapz(f,S) exactly and "ifft" and "direct" agree to rounding error (original help). The "direct" R(0) is NUFFT-accurate, about 1e-12, and arma2acv equals spec2acv(arma2psd(...)) at integer lags to about 1e-15 on a fine grid (sigtools HANDOFF sections 3 and 3a). Resampling and zero-padding add the interpolation error of the input grid. Unchanged from sigtools v1.3.1; TestSigtoolsEquivalence holds it bit for bit.

Example

Auto-correlation of a 4th-order low-pass PSD, with uncertainty.

fs = 1000; f = (0:0.5:fs/2)';
S  = 1./(1 + (f/50).^4);
[R, tau, nfo] = timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.spec2acv(f, S, Window = "tukey", Smooth = "octave", SmoothWidth = 1/6, DOF = 32, Output = "acf");

Limitations

The spectrum is assumed zero outside the given band, so an undersampled low-frequency end biases long lags and R(0). Text options are case-sensitive (v1.3.1). The uncertainty assumes independent chi-square bins before smoothing.
