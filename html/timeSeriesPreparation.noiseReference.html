timeSeriesPreparation.noiseReference

Published noise ASDs of the GRACE-FO instruments: LRI requirement, KBR, LRI laser, ACC.

Syntax

[asd, info] = timeSeriesPreparation.noiseReference(model, f)
[asd, info] = timeSeriesPreparation.noiseReference("ACC", f, Axis = "y", Derivative = 0)

Description

The reference noise-model library of the noise stage (CONCEPT 6.1, row 5; W-12). These are amplitude spectral densities (ASD, one-sided) as printed in the sources, to be used as priors and as validation targets for a fitted noise model (noise, spectral.spec2acv). Each formula below was read from the page image of the cited copy on 2026-09-23: "LRI" the GRACE-FO LRI ranging-noise REQUIREMENT, in m/sqrt(Hz): 80e-9 sqrt(1 + (3 mHz/f)^2) sqrt(1 + (10 mHz/f)^2), stated for 2 mHz <= f <= 100 mHz (Abich et al. 2019, arXiv:1907.00104v1 p. 4, the unnumbered display). "LRI-laser" the LRI laser-frequency ranging noise of the simulation model, for a separation of 238 km, in m/sqrt(Hz): 5e-9 sqrt(1 + (0.0182 Hz/f)^2) (Darbeheshti et al. 2017, Eq. 21, p. 841). The equation states no band; info.band is the range its Figure 12 plots, 1e-4 to 1e-1 Hz. "KBR" the KBR system and oscillator range noise of the simulation model, for a separation of 238 km, in m/sqrt(Hz): 1e-6 sqrt(1 + (0.0018 Hz/f)^4), stated for 1e-5 <= f <= 1e-1 Hz (Darbeheshti et al. 2017, Eq. 15, p. 839, after Kim 2000). "ACC" the accelerometer noise of the simulation model, in m s^-2/sqrt(Hz): sensitive axes x and z 1e-10 sqrt(1 + 0.005 Hz/f), less-sensitive axis y 1e-9 sqrt(1 + 0.1 Hz/f), stated for 1e-5 <= f <= 1e-1 Hz (Darbeheshti et al. 2017, Eq. 9, p. 838, after Kim 2000). Axes are the science frame (SF). Derivative n multiplies by (2 pi f)^n: range to range rate (1) and range acceleration (2), the ASD of the n-th time derivative of a stationary process.

These are models and a requirement, not measurements. For scale: the in-orbit LRI reached about 1 nm/sqrt(Hz) above 100 mHz (Abich et al. 2019, abstract), well below its requirement. The KBR-LRI residuals show a KBR white-noise level of about 0.6 um/sqrt(Hz) (Mueller et al. 2022, sect. 6.3), below the model's 1 um/sqrt(Hz).

Accuracy

Exact evaluation of the printed formulas. TestNoiseReference pins each at its corner frequencies, where the printed constants give closed forms (e.g. KBR at 1.8 mHz is sqrt(2) um/sqrt(Hz)), to 1e-15 relative. The formulas themselves carry no stated uncertainty.

Example

The LRI requirement and the KBR model from 1 to 100 mHz.

f = logspace(-3, -1, 50)';
lri = timeSeriesPreparation.noiseReference("LRI", f);
[kbr, info] = timeSeriesPreparation.noiseReference("KBR", f);

Limitations

Outside info.band the formulas are evaluated as printed; the source does not state them there (info.inBand is false). "KBR" and "LRI-laser" hold for a separation of 238 km; the other separations are not modelled. The requirement is an upper bound on the noise, not a model of it. The GRACE (classic) instruments are not covered.
