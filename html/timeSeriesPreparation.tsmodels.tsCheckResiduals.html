timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.tsCheckResiduals

Residual diagnostics of a fitted ARIMA/SARIMA model: ACF, Ljung-Box, Jarque-Bera.

Syntax

out = timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.tsCheckResiduals(model)
out = timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.tsCheckResiduals(model, lags)

Description

The numbers behind the standard residual check of a model from tsFitArima and its wrappers: - the standardised residuals; - the residual ACF with its white-noise band; - the Ljung-Box p-value ladder over lags 1..L, with degrees of freedom L minus the number of ARMA parameters; - the Jarque-Bera normality test (chi-square, 2 dof); - the normal Q-Q pairs. Since X-02 it draws nothing. plotResiduals draws the four-panel figure from this output (owner decision 2026-09-23, clean break). The numbers are v1.3.1's; the output lost the field figure and gained the Q-Q data qqSample and qqTheoretical, which v1.3.1 computed only for the plot. Base MATLAB only.

Inputs

namesizetypedefaultdescription
model1,1struct—- From tsFitArima, tsFitAR, tsFitMA, tsFitARMA or tsAutoArima; uses resid, obs, p, q, and P, Q for SARIMA.
lags1,1double—- Largest lag of the ACF and the ladder; [] or omitted: min(20, floor(nObs/4)).

Outputs

namesizetypedefaultdescription
out1,1struct—- Fields: resid (N,1) standardised residuals, NaN at gaps; acf (L,1), lags 1..L; acfConf, the +/- white-noise band; lags and lbLags (L,1); lbP and lbQ (L,1), the Ljung-Box p-values and statistics; jbStat, jbP; qqSample (nObs,1), the sorted standardised residuals; qqTheoretical (nObs,1), the normal quantiles at (k - 0.5)/nObs.

Accuracy

The chi-square CDF comes from a series and continued-fraction incomplete gamma, converged to 1e-14 relative. The normal quantiles use the Acklam rational approximation, whose relative error is below 1.15e-9. The numbers are unchanged from v1.3.1, and TestSigtoolsEquivalence holds them bit for bit.

Errors

(none) A model without the field resid, or a lags that is not a scalar, is refused by the arguments block or by MATLAB with a MATLAB: identifier.

Example

An AR(1) fit and its residual numbers.

x = filter(1, [1 -0.6], randn(RandStream("twister", "Seed", 2), 500, 1));
out = timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.tsCheckResiduals(timeSeriesPreparation.tsmodels.tsFitAR(x, 1));

Limitations

The Ljung-Box degrees of freedom subtract p + q (+ P + Q); for lags below that count they are clamped to 1, so the first p-values are not meaningful.

References

G. M. Ljung, G. E. P. Box (1978), Biometrika 65(2), 297-303. C. M. Jarque, A. K. Bera (1980), Econ. Lett. 6(3), 255-259. P. J. Acklam (2003), An algorithm for computing the inverse normal cumulative distribution function.
