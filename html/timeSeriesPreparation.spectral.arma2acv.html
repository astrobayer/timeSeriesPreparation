timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.arma2acv

Exact auto-covariance / -correlation of a discrete-time ARMA process.

Syntax

[R, lags] = timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.arma2acv(b, a, maxLag)
[R, lags, info] = timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.arma2acv(b, a, maxLag, NoiseVariance = 2, Output = "acf", Fs = 10)

Description

Returns the theoretical auto-covariance R(0..maxLag) of the stationary ARMA process x[n] = filter(b, a, w[n]), w white with variance sigma_w^2. Together with arma2psd and spec2acv it closes the spectral <-> time-series loop: arma2acv(b, a, ...) and spec2acv(arma2psd(b, a, ...)) agree to quadrature accuracy.

The default method is exact. The ARMA(p,q) is written in companion state-space form, T = [phi, I; 0] (r x r, r = max(p, q+1)), with noise loading R = [1; theta_1..theta_q; 0]. The stationary state covariance solves the discrete Lyapunov equation P = T P T' + sigma_w^2 R R' (doubling iteration, tolerance 1e-15, 80 steps, Kronecker fallback; timeSeriesPreparation.internal.dlyap), and gamma(k) = e1' T^k P e1, since the first state is x[n]. There is no truncation error, and R(1) is the exact process variance. Method "psi" instead sums the MA(inf) weights, gamma(k) = sigma_w^2 sum_j psi_j psi_(j+k), truncated at PsiTerms terms; by default so many that the slowest pole has decayed to 1e-6 in amplitude (about 1e-12 of R(0) in gamma), at least maxLag+50, at most 1e5.

b and a are normalised by a(1). A root of A with |z| >= 1 - 1e-12 is refused as non-stationary.

Accuracy

sigtools HANDOFF section 3 (NumPy re-derivation): the Lyapunov and psi methods agree to about 1e-15 across AR(1), MA(2), ARMA(2,1) and ARMA(1,3); the AR(1) and MA(q) closed forms are exact; arma2acv equals spec2acv(arma2psd(...)) at integer lags to about 1e-15 on a fine grid; the doubling Lyapunov solve matches the Kronecker solve to about 1e-15. Unchanged from sigtools v1.3.1; TestSigtoolsEquivalence holds it bit for bit.

Example

Auto-covariance of an ARMA(2,1) process up to lag 30.

[R, k, info] = timeSeriesPreparation.spectral.arma2acv([1 -0.5], [1 -1.2 0.6], 30, NoiseVariance = 2);

Limitations

Stationary models only; no integrated (ARIMA) or seasonal differencing. Method "psi" is approximate for poles near the unit circle (truncation at 1e5 terms). info.integralTime is a finite sum up to maxLag, not the infinite integral.
